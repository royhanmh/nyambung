# nyambung — Brand Guidelines

## Brand
nyambung

Tagline: Biar ngobrol tetap nyambung.

## Product identity
Mobile-first AI conversation game for natural Indonesian conversations.
The product should feel like a native smartphone app delivered through the web.

## Personality
- Hangat
- Playful
- Peka
- Nyata
- Minimalis
- Human

## Tone of voice
Use natural, conversational Bahasa Indonesia.
Prefer everyday phrasing over formal or textbook Indonesian.
Use slang selectively and naturally; never force slang into every sentence.
Avoid robotic, corporate, motivational, or overly philosophical wording.

Good:
"Kalau besok kamu bebas seharian, mau ngapain?"

Avoid:
"Apa aktivitas yang akan Anda lakukan apabila memiliki waktu luang selama satu hari?"

## Visual direction
- Minimalist
- Soft pastel accents
- Warm neutral backgrounds
- Rounded shapes
- Generous whitespace
- Friendly typography
- Light illustrations/icons
- Avoid visual clutter, neon-heavy palettes, excessive gradients, and generic SaaS dashboard styling.

## Core colors
Pastel peach, soft pink, lavender, mint, butter yellow, neutral gray, and dark slate.
Use one pastel accent at a time for important interactions.

## Typography
Primary recommendation: Plus Jakarta Sans.
Use bold/semi-bold for headings and question text; regular/medium for supporting UI.

## Logo
Preferred: lowercase "nyambung" wordmark with the connected speech-bubble mark.
Do not stretch, skew, recolor outside the approved palette, or add arbitrary effects.
Keep generous clear space around the mark.

## Responsive behavior
Smartphone is the source of truth.
Do not introduce a persistent desktop sidebar.
On desktop, constrain the main experience to a mobile-oriented width and use surrounding whitespace.
The app should feel like a mobile app that happens to work on the web.

## AI question writing
AI-generated questions must:
- sound naturally Indonesian
- vary sentence structure
- avoid repetitive "Apa..." / "Mengapa..." patterns
- avoid translationese
- adapt to the conversation context
- be concise enough for a phone card
- sometimes be funny, casual, surprising, or reflective
- prioritize conversation over sounding profound

## Gesture language
- Swipe left: Lewati
- Swipe right: Lanjut
- Swipe up: Lebih dalam
- Swipe down: Santai dulu
- Long press: Tanya lagi / follow-up
- Tap/hold: Reveal when relevant
- Shake: optional Chaos mode

## Asset guidance
Use the provided PNG assets as raster references. For production, recreate the logo as clean vector artwork rather than tracing the brand-board screenshot.
