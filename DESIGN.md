# Nyambung Design Direction

## Design read

Nyambung is a small Indonesian social ritual on a phone: warm, tactile,
slightly playful, and calm enough to leave room for conversation.

## Tokens

- Background: #f5efe8
- Surface: #fffdfb
- Ink: #17423d
- Muted: #536a68
- Accent: #557872
- Accent dark: #3d5a53
- Accent soft: #e6efea
- Coral: #f4513f
- Coral dark: #c83c30

Dark mode keeps the same hierarchy: deep green background, warm text, muted
secondary text, green action, and coral only for meaningful emphasis.

## Shape and surface

- Question card is the main elevated object.
- Choices and settings use quiet rows or compact controls.
- Navigation stays text-led.
- Use radius values around 12, 14, and 22px only.
- Use shadows only for the question surface and overlays.
- Avoid nested cards, glass everywhere, decorative borders, and card mosaics.

## Type and spacing

- Nunito carries questions, headings, and primary actions.
- Plus Jakarta Sans carries supporting UI and body copy.
- Use 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, and 48px spacing relationships.
- Keep labels short. Avoid technical AI language.
- Bahasa Indonesia is default. Slang stays selective and natural.

## Interaction

- The question is handed between people, not browsed like a database.
- Swipe feedback should be brief and directional.
- Favorite state uses a clear heart control.
- Motion stays restrained: no bounce, glow loops, or perpetual animation.
- Every control must work, or it must not ship.
- Touch targets stay at least 44px.

## Anti-slop locks

- No sidebar or dashboard shell.
- No purple, cyan, neon, or generic AI badge.
- No repeated rounded cards for every section.
- No decorative emoji system.
- No fake metrics, fake social proof, or dead navigation.
- No English product labels when a natural Indonesian label exists.
